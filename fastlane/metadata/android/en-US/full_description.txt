
Pocket Notes is a simple and lightweight offline note-taking application.

FEATURES

• Create personal notes
• Edit existing notes
• Delete unwanted notes
• Search notes by title or content
• Switch between light and dark themes
• Automatically save notes on your device

PRIVACY

• No account registration required
• No internet connection required
• No advertisements
• No analytics tracking
• Notes are stored locally on your device

Pocket Notes helps you organize your thoughts and important information with a clean and simple interface.
